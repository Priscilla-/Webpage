Outreach

Alongside research and teaching, I take part in outreach and mentoring. The activities below range from masterclasses and workshops for school pupils to mentoring women in science and engineering and assessing postgraduate scholarship applications.

Masterclasses and schools

  • Royal Institution Masterclasses
    Mathematics and physics masterclasses for primary-school children (2020–2025)
  • NRICH
    Outreach activities with the University of Cambridge's mathematics enrichment project
  • Form the Future
    Workshops and STEM talks for secondary-school students

Mentoring and scholarships

  • Martingale Foundation
    Assessor of applications for the Foundation's fully funded postgraduate scholarships for students from low-income backgrounds
  • CamAWiSE
    Mentoring through the Cambridge Association for Women in Science and Engineering
