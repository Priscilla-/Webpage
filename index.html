Welcome to my webpage! I work on gravitational-wave source modelling and data analysis for compact binaries. I'm also interested in applied mathematics and scientific machine learning, especially methods that are principled and interpretable. Beyond research, I'm active in outreach and mentoring, from Royal Institution masterclasses to STEM workshops in schools.

Research

Waveform modelling and data analysis for compact binaries: eccentric signals, extreme-mass-ratio inspirals, and reduced-order methods for fast Bayesian inference.

Publications

Selected papers, preprints, and projects, arranged so visitors can find the signal instead of spelunking through a citation cave.

Teaching

Lectures, supervision, and coursework design spanning scientific machine learning, inverse problems, and image analysis.
